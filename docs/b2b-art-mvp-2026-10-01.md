# B2B Art & Licensing MVP — 1.10.2026

> Goal: validate demand from offices, clinics, hospitality and interior-design clients before building a complex B2B sales system.

## Hypothesis

A single qualified B2B deal can be materially more valuable than many low-ticket consumer purchases. The MVP should therefore test interest with a simple, high-trust inquiry flow rather than a full quote engine or account system.

## Three offer packages

### 1. Digital Display License
For offices, clinics, restaurants, hospitality and commercial spaces that want photography for screens, websites or internal digital displays.

Includes:
- selected image or small image set
- commercial-use license scoped to the agreed channels
- digital delivery
- quote based on use scope

### 2. Wall Art Selection
For spaces that want a curated set of images for physical display.

Includes:
- 3–10 image shortlist
- basic curation by theme / room / atmosphere
- optional print-production path later
- no automated Gelato promise in MVP

### 3. Custom Collection
For interior designers, hospitality or multi-location clients.

Includes:
- tailored image shortlist
- project-level usage rights
- possibility for regional / Israel / nature / travel themes
- quote based on number of images, locations and usage

## MVP CTA

One primary CTA: "Request a business art quote".

The inquiry should ask only for:
- business/company name
- contact name
- email
- intended use
- approximate number of images / spaces
- optional image IDs or links

Do not collect unnecessary PII.

## Measurement

GA events:
- `b2b_intent`
- `b2b_package_select` with safe label: `digital_display | wall_art | custom_collection`
- `b2b_contact_start`
- `contact_form_success` (existing success event if available)

Do not send company name, email, free-text message, phone or project details to GA.

## Qualification

A lead is considered qualified if it has:
- identifiable business context
- concrete use case
- at least one requested image/space/use
- a way to follow up

## Success threshold

First 14–28 day read is directional only.

A B2B signal is meaningful if any of these occur:
- 3+ qualified inbound inquiries
- 10–20 targeted outreach messages produce 2+ real replies
- one request reaches quote stage

Do not build CRM, automated pricing or production workflow before that.

## Pricing

Do not publish rigid B2B prices in MVP.

Reason:
- usage scope varies widely
- value is tied to usage, locations and distribution
- publishing low fixed prices may anchor enterprise buyers too low

Use "tailored quote" until enough real inquiries reveal the common patterns.

## Gelato relationship

Gelato is optional fulfillment for physical wall art, not part of the B2B core proposition.

Decision sequence:
1. validate B2B demand
2. learn requested products / sizes / locations
3. calculate margin and quality needs
4. then decide whether Gelato fits fulfillment

## Non-goals

- no CRM
- no automated B2B checkout
- no subscription
- no custom print configurator
- no production payment activation
- no Gelato commitment
